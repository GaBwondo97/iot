# CityPulse AI — Project Overview

## What CityPulse AI Does
CityPulse AI is a global AI-powered urban monitoring and risk assessment decision-support system. It combines public APIs, simulated IoT measurements, and AI-driven risk scoring to provide insights into environmental and urban conditions across global cities.

## Problems It Aims to Address
- **Urban environmental monitoring** — Air quality, weather, traffic, and population data is fragmented across different sources.
- **Risk assessment** — Cities need automated, explainable risk scoring for environmental conditions.
- **Decision support** — Urban planners, emergency services, and citizens need actionable insights, not raw data.

## Main System Capabilities
1. **Environmental Monitoring** — Temperature, humidity, rainfall, wind speed, PM2.5, PM10, AQI, CO2, traffic density, and population metrics.
2. **Risk Scoring** — Rule-based risk assessment with explainable confidence levels and severity ratings.
3. **Anomaly Detection** — Statistical anomaly detection against rolling baselines.
4. **Alerting** — Active alerts triggered by risk thresholds.
5. **Recommendations** — Explainable recommendations generated from scoring drivers.

## Intended Users
- Urban planners and city officials
- Emergency services
- Environmental researchers
- Citizens seeking air quality and traffic information

## MVP Scope
- **Pilot Cities:** Kuala Lumpur, London, Tokyo
- **Monitoring Categories:** Weather, Air Quality, Traffic, Population
- **Data Sources:** Simulated IoT sensors, Public APIs (Open-Meteo, OpenAQ)
- **Risk Scoring:** Rule-based with statistical anomaly detection
- **Frontend:** Dashboard with city selection, monitoring overview, and risk assessment display

## What Is NOT in MVP
- Real-time emergency response integration
- Machine learning model training (optional comparison only)
- Physical IoT hardware
- Production-grade deployment
